History & backups

Daily logs and your library are saved on the NAS in SQLite. Back up the NAS data folder regularly. Export nutrient details CSV for per-supplement ingredients, servings, and dose amounts; export daily totals CSV for combined nutrients per date. The JSON backup button provides an additional portable backup.

Supplement library

Edit a supplement's name, brand, and ingredient rows using JSON. Back up before changes. Past logged nutrient snapshots are preserved even when the product is edited.

Inactive and discontinued products are hidden from new daily checklists, but remain in the library, historical logs, and CSV exports.

Import supplements

Paste supplement facts text or upload CSV/XLSX. Preview and edit ingredients before adding anything to your library. Nothing is saved until you choose Add to library.

Use columns: Supplement, Brand, Nutrient, Amount, Unit, Type. Supports several supplements per file. XLSX: first worksheet only, values only. Maximum 2 MB.

Review before import

Amounts are per serving. Verify units, serving size, and proprietary blends against the original product label. Duplicate product names are skipped unless you intentionally replace them.
